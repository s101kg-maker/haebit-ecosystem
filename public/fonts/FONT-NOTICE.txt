Haebit Gothic is derived from Noto Sans KR distributed by @fontsource/noto-sans-kr.
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see LICENSE.txt.
The per-Unicode webfont subsets were merged, retaining the original glyph outlines.
Layout tables were removed during merging; family renamed to Haebit Gothic.
Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk

Dispatch Head, Dispatch Round, and Dispatch Reading are derived respectively from Do Hyeon, Jua, and Gowun Dodum distributed by Fontsource.
Per-Unicode subsets were merged without changing glyph outlines, layout tables removed, and internal font families renamed.
Each original font's SIL OFL 1.1 license and copyright notice are included in the matching *-LICENSE.txt file.
Preview fonts are subsets of these derived fonts for the sample interface.

Haebit Document Serif is derived from Gowun Batang Bold distributed by Fontsource.
Original glyph outlines retained; per-Unicode subsets merged, layout tables removed, internal font family renamed.
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see gowun-batang-LICENSE.txt.
Source: https://github.com/yangheeryu/Gowun-Batang
